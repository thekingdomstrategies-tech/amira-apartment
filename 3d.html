Place real-size pieces and check the walkways

Pick a piece and it lands on a clear spot near the middle of your view. Drag to move (5 cm steps); a face within 8 cm of a wall or piece closes up against it (hold Alt to place freely). The arrows nudge it 5 cm (Shift 1 cm), R turns it 90° (Shift+R 15°), Delete removes it, Ctrl+Z (⌘Z) undoes. Type a width and depth to size it. Lines show the gap on each side; under 75 cm turns amber. When you're happy, tell me and I draw the layout into the plan page and make it this plan's starting layout. Each plan keeps its own layout.
